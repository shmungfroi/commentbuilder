## A. Remove "…as part of senior music study"

- **Rule:** the phrase is removed only where it ends a sentence; nothing else changes.
- **Effect:** each affected Stage 6 comment gets 30–90 characters shorter.
- **What stays:** 110 sentences still *open* with "Approaches senior music study…". Those are untouched apart from losing the repeated ending.

Example (Music 2 HSC Viva Voce, Thorough):

> **Before (539 characters):** Interprets mandatory topic repertoire with effective attention to style, performance craft and musical intention, connecting technical preparation to expressive decision-making. Presents mandatory topic repertoire viva voce material with effective preparation, focused examples and clear links between repertoire, context and concepts of music. Approaches senior music study with secure organisation, consistent preparation and a developing sense of artistic responsibility across practical and written tasks as part of senior music study.
>
> **After (514 characters):** Interprets repertoire from the mandatory topic with effective attention to style, performance craft and musical intention, connecting technical preparation to expressive decision-making. Presents viva voce material on the mandatory topic with effective preparation, focused examples and clear links between repertoire, context and concepts of music. Approaches senior music study with secure organisation, consistent preparation and a developing sense of artistic responsibility across practical and written tasks.

## B. Placeholder topic labels → plain wording

The topic stays distinct (Music 2 mandatory vs additional), but reads as report language. Ten sentence patterns × four labels cover all the affected records.

|---|---|---|
| HSC elective topic | the HSC topics | Music 1 HSC |
| mandatory topic repertoire | the mandatory topic | Music 2 HSC |
| additional topic repertoire | the additional topic | Music 2 HSC |

| Pattern now (X = label) | Proposed (Y = new wording) |
|---|---|
| Examines {X} with | Examines repertoire from {Y} with |

| Strengthen {X} performance preparation by | Strengthen performance preparation for {Y} by |
| Strengthen {X} viva voce preparation by | Strengthen viva voce preparation for {Y} by |

- **Recommendation:** this is neutral wording that works for every term. Named topics (e.g. An Instrument and its Repertoire) would be **new** records, added later as step 4.
- **Watch out:** three topic-specific sentences together still repeat "repertoire from the HSC topics". Mixing in a standard sentence reads better.
