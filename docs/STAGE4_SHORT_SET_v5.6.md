**What:**
- Three sentences (Performance / Composition / Listening) are kept. Every new sentence is **48 characters or fewer**, so any comment built from them is at most 146 characters.
- Written from the unit summaries (Beats & Tunes, Rockin' the Guitar, Sound Lab, Jazz It Up), with wording that genuinely steps down by tier.
- 35 short General strategies, so the General swap also fits.

**How the app uses it:**

**To change a sentence:** tell me which one and the new wording. Anything you change stays within 48 characters.
